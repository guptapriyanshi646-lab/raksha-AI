REMEMBER THIS

Scammers create urgency.
You can create a pause.

Government agencies and banks should not pressure you to transfer money to “verify” your account. Stop, independently contact the organization using an official channel, and talk to someone you trust.

Never share OTPs, PINs, passwords, or screen access with an unknown caller. If someone threatens you, end the call and verify independently.
